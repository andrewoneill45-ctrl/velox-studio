# Endurance exercise performance: the physiology of champions

**Citation:** Joyner MJ; Coyle EF (2008). *The Journal of Physiology*.  
**Type:** Foundational review  
**Population:** Elite endurance performance  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2007.143834

## What it adds
Endurance performance can be understood through interacting determinants including VO2max, sustainable fraction/threshold and economy/efficiency, shaped by training and morphology.

## App implication
Model performance as multidimensional. A rider can improve through VO2max, threshold/critical power, efficiency, anaerobic capacity and durability—not FTP alone.

## Tags
VO2max, threshold, economy, foundations

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
