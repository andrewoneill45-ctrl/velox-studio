# Sleep and the athlete: narrative review and 2021 expert consensus recommendations

**Citation:** Walsh NP; Halson SL; Sargent C; et al. (2021). *British Journal of Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Expert consensus + narrative review  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2020-102025

## What it adds
Athletes have individual sleep needs and are vulnerable to sleep restriction. Sleep extension, naps and sleep-hygiene strategies can be useful when individualized.

## App implication
Track sleep opportunity and perceived sleep quality, not just wearable stages. Flag repeated short sleep and adjust demanding sessions where performance/readiness is clearly impaired.

## Tags
sleep, recovery, readiness

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
