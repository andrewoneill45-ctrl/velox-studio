# Effects of different interval-training programs on cycling time-trial performance

**Citation:** Stepto NK; Hawley JA; Dennis SC; Hopkins WG (1999). *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*.  
**Type:** Cycling intervention  
**Population:** Trained cyclists  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1097/00005768-199905000-00018

## What it adds
Different high-intensity interval formats can improve time-trial performance, reinforcing the importance of matching interval design to the athlete and target.

## App implication
Maintain a library of distinct interval types rather than one generic VO2 workout.

## Tags
cycling, intervals, time-trial

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
