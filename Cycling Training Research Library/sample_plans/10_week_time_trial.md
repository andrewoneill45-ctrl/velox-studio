# 10-week time-trial plan

Priorities: sustainable aerodynamic power, threshold/critical-power development, VO2 support and position durability.

- **Tue:** threshold progression (e.g. 3×10 → 2×20 → 3×15)
- **Thu:** VO2 (e.g. 5×4 / 4×5) or over-unders alternating by week
- **Sat:** 2–3.5 h low-intensity endurance with 20–40 min in TT position
- **Sun:** easy endurance
- **Strength:** 2× first 4 weeks, then 1× maintenance
- **Position exposure:** short frequent TT-position blocks rather than one huge uncomfortable session
- **Week 4/8:** reduced-load weeks
- **Week 10:** taper and race/test

Use position-specific power and comfort: the best laboratory FTP is irrelevant if the rider cannot sustain it aerodynamically.
