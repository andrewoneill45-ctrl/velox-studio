# Factors associated with overuse injury in cyclists: A systematic review

**Citation:** Multiple authors (2022). *Journal of Science and Medicine in Sport*.  
**Type:** Systematic review  
**Population:** Cyclists; 18 included studies  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsams.2021.12.008

## What it adds
Evidence linking many specific bike/body-fit measures to overuse injury is weak; previous symptoms and load-related factors have more consistent support than simplistic fit rules.

## App implication
Do not diagnose pain from saddle height/knee angle formulas. Use load change and symptom history as core flags, then recommend qualified bike-fit/clinical assessment where needed.

## Tags
injury, bike-fit, load

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
