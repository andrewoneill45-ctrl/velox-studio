# Effects of tapering on performance in endurance athletes: A systematic review and meta-analysis

**Citation:** Wang Z; Wang Y; Gao W; Zhong Y (2023). *PLOS ONE*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 14 endurance studies  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0282838

## What it adds
Tapering improves time-trial/time-to-exhaustion performance. The best-supported approach reduces volume about 41–60%, keeps intensity and frequency, and lasts up to 21 days.

## App implication
For priority events, create 7–14 day default tapers (extend toward 21 days for large accumulated load) with ~40–60% volume reduction while retaining short intensity exposures.

## Tags

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
