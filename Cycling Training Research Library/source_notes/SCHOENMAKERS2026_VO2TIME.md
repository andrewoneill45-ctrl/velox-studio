# Time spent at or near V̇O₂max during high-intensity interval training - a systematic review and meta-analysis

**Citation:** Schoenmakers P; Murray K; White J; Matta G; Bossi AH (2026). *BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 86 articles; 239 HIIT protocols  
**Directness:** HIIT across endurance modalities  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-026-01766-x

## What it adds
Long intervals of at least ~2 minutes generally generate more accumulated time near VO2max than short/moderate intervals; variable-intensity work can increase time near VO2max.

## App implication
When the goal is VO2 stimulus, prioritize repeatable 2–5 minute intervals or carefully designed variable intervals rather than treating any hard workout as equivalent.

## Tags
HIIT, VO2max, interval-duration

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
