# Cycling physiology and testing

## Performance model
Do not reduce cycling performance to one number. Useful dimensions are:
- maximal aerobic power / VO2max-related ability;
- sustainable metabolic power (critical power / maximal steady-state concepts; field threshold estimates);
- economy/efficiency;
- anaerobic work capacity and sprint power;
- power-duration profile;
- durability/fatigue resistance after accumulated work.

## Testing stack
### Minimum viable field stack
- best recent 5 s, 1 min, 5 min, 20 min power from valid maximal/near-maximal efforts;
- standardized 20-min or longer threshold-style field test if desired;
- repeatable endurance ride with HR-to-power decoupling;
- subjective RPE and session-RPE.

### Better stack
Use 3–4 maximal efforts of different durations on separate days or with sufficient recovery to fit a power-duration / critical-power model. Re-test every ~6–10 weeks rather than chasing daily “FTP updates”.

### Durability
For long-event athletes, periodically compare fresh 5–20 min performance with the same effort after a standardized amount of prior work (e.g. accumulated kJ or duration below threshold). Standardize fuelling, temperature and route/trainer.

## FTP implementation
FTP is useful because athletes understand it and platforms use it. It should be presented as a practical performance estimate, not a direct measurement of lactate threshold or maximal metabolic steady state. Use it for familiar UI zones while maintaining a deeper physiological model underneath.
