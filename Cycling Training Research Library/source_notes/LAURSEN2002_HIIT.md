# The Scientific Basis for High-Intensity Interval Training: Optimising Training Programmes and Maximising Performance in Highly Trained Endurance Athletes

**Citation:** Laursen PB; Jenkins DG (2002). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Foundational review  
**Population:** Highly trained endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.2165/00007256-200232010-00003

## What it adds
HIIT is a potent tool for trained endurance athletes but must be carefully dosed and integrated with lower-intensity work.

## App implication
Do not turn the app into a HIIT generator. High-intensity sessions are a minority tool inside a sustainable programme.

## Tags
HIIT, foundations

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
