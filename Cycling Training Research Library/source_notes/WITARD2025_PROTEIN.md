# Protein Nutrition for Endurance Athletes: A Metabolic Focus on Promoting Recovery and Training Adaptation

**Citation:** Witard OC; Hearris M; Morgan PT (2025). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Review  
**Population:** Endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-025-02203-8

## What it adds
Endurance athletes may benefit from protein intakes toward the upper end of traditional sports recommendations, especially under heavy load, recovery or low-carbohydrate availability.

## App implication
Use a broad evidence-aware protein range rather than a rigid value; distribute protein across meals and prioritize sufficient total energy/carbohydrate first.

## Tags
protein, recovery, nutrition

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
