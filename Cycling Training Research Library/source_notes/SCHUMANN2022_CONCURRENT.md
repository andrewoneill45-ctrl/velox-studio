# Compatibility of Concurrent Aerobic and Strength Training for Skeletal Muscle Size and Function: An Updated Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Citation:** Schumann M; Feuerbacher JF; Sünkeler M; et al. (2022). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 43 studies; healthy adults  
**Directness:** General concurrent training; cycling subgroup assessed  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7

## What it adds
Concurrent endurance + strength generally does not compromise hypertrophy or maximal strength, but explosive strength gains can be attenuated, especially when both are performed in the same session.

## App implication
Separate key strength and key endurance sessions by several hours or different days when possible. If same day, order by the primary adaptation goal and keep the second session low-cost.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
