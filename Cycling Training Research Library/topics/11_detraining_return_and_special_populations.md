# Detraining, return-to-training and special populations

## Return after a break
Fitness losses begin with training cessation and become broader as the break lengthens. Do not reactivate the previous plan at full load.

Use a 2–4+ week re-entry phase depending on time off and reason:
- restore frequency first;
- mostly easy riding initially;
- reintroduce moderate work before maximal work;
- reintroduce strength with technique/submaximal loads before heavy loading;
- re-test only after enough training to make the result meaningful.

Illness/injury returns require condition-specific medical guidance.

## Female athletes
Do not prescribe training automatically by predicted menstrual-cycle phase. Track symptoms and individual responses; clinically significant symptoms should be assessed.

## Masters athletes
Preserve high-intensity exposure as tolerated and give strength, protein distribution and recovery greater attention with age. Age alone should not force all training to become easy; recovery capacity and health status should drive individualization.
