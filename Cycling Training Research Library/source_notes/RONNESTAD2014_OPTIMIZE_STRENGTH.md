# Optimizing strength training for running and cycling endurance performance: A review

**Citation:** Rønnestad BR; Mujika I (2014). *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*.  
**Type:** Review  
**Population:** Endurance runners and cyclists  
**Directness:** Cycling + running  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.12104

## What it adds
Heavy strength training can improve endurance performance determinants through neuromuscular and efficiency-related mechanisms, with appropriate integration into endurance training.

## App implication
Prioritize maximal strength and neural quality over bodybuilding-style fatigue. Use long rests and avoid routine lifting to failure.

## Tags
strength, concurrent, economy

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
