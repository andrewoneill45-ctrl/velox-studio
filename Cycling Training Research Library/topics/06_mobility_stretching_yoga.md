# Mobility, stretching and yoga

## What the evidence supports
- Static stretching reliably improves range of motion over time.
- Mobility training can improve ROM and selected sport-performance measures, but evidence is heterogeneous.
- Yoga has promising evidence for flexibility, balance and wellbeing in athletes, but direct cycling-performance evidence is sparse.

## App position
These are **adjuncts**, not primary cycling fitness builders.

### Dynamic pre-ride / pre-strength mobility (5–8 min)
- hip flexor dynamic lunge
- leg swings
- ankle rocks
- bodyweight squat
- thoracic rotations

### Post-ride or separate mobility (10–20 min)
- hip flexor stretch
- glute/piriformis stretch if comfortable
- calf stretch
- hamstring stretch
- thoracic extension/rotation
- chest/shoulder opening for aero-position riders

### Yoga (20–40 min, optional 1–3×/week)
Use lower-intensity flows focused on mobility, breathing, balance and relaxation. Avoid placing demanding yoga sessions before priority cycling/strength workouts if they create soreness/fatigue.

## Important limitation
Do not claim stretching or yoga prevents cycling injury or raises FTP unless future direct evidence supports that claim.
