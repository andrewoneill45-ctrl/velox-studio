# 2023 International Olympic Committee’s consensus statement on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs)

**Citation:** Mountjoy M; Ackerman KE; Bailey DM; et al. (2023). *British Journal of Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** IOC consensus statement  
**Population:** Athletes of all sexes  
**Directness:** Athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994

## What it adds
Problematic low energy availability can impair health and performance in athletes of all sexes; low carbohydrate availability can contribute to risk.

## App implication
Never encourage chronic under-fuelling for weight loss or “train low” without safeguards. Add RED-S warning flags and refer persistent symptoms to sports medicine/dietetic professionals.

## Tags
RED-S, energy-availability, health

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
