# Application of mobility training methods in sporting populations: A systematic review of performance adaptations

**Citation:** Skopal LK; Drinkwater EJ; Behm DG (2024). *Journal of Sports Sciences*.  
**Type:** Systematic review  
**Population:** 22 sports studies  
**Directness:** Sporting populations; indirect to cycling  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2024.2321006

## What it adds
Mobility interventions can improve range of motion and selected physical-performance measures, but methods and outcomes vary.

## App implication
Offer short mobility routines based on measured restriction or rider comfort, not mandatory generic mobility volume for everyone.

## Tags
mobility, ROM, performance

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
