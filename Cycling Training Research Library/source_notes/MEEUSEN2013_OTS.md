# Prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of the overtraining syndrome: joint consensus statement of the ECSS and ACSM

**Type:** Consensus statement  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e318279a10a

## What it adds
Functional overreaching, non-functional overreaching and overtraining syndrome are clinically distinct concepts; persistent performance decline requires exclusion of illness and other causes.

## App implication
Do not diagnose overtraining from an app. Escalate persistent unexplained performance decline, fatigue, mood/sleep disturbance or illness symptoms to a qualified clinician.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
