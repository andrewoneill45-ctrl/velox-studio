# Heavy strength training effects on physiological determinants of endurance cyclist performance: a systematic review with meta-analysis

**Citation:** Llanos-Lagos C; Ramirez-Campillo R; Sáez de Villarreal E (2025). *European Journal of Applied Physiology*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 17 studies, 262 cyclists  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-025-05883-2

## What it adds
Heavy strength training (≥80% 1RM) improved cycling efficiency, anaerobic power and cycling performance, without clear effects on VO2max. Benefits appear after sufficient duration/dose.

## App implication
For healthy riders, use 2 strength sessions/week in development blocks, centred on heavy lower-body compound lifts; maintain with ~1 session/week when cycling intensity/competition rises.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
