# Durability as an independent parameter of endurance performance in cycling

**Citation:** Barsumyan A; Soost C; Shyla R; et al. (2025). *BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation*.  
**Type:** Cycling study  
**Population:** 20 well-trained amateur road cyclists  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-025-01238-8

## What it adds
After 1000 kJ of prior work, 5- and 20-min power fell by about 10% on average; durability was not clearly related to VO2max or relative FTP.

## App implication
Treat durability as a separate performance dimension. Include “fresh vs fatigued power” tests for long-event athletes rather than assuming a high FTP means strong late-race performance.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
