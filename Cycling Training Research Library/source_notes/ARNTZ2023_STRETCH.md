# Chronic Effects of Static Stretching Exercises on Muscle Strength and Power in Healthy Individuals Across the Lifespan: A Systematic Review with Multi-level Meta-analysis

**Citation:** Arntz F; Markov A; Behm DG; et al. (2023). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 41 controlled studies  
**Directness:** General population/athletes; indirect to cycling  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01806-9

## What it adds
Long-term static stretching improves flexibility and may produce trivial-to-small positive strength/power effects; it is not inherently harmful when programmed appropriately.

## App implication
Use stretching mainly for range of motion and comfort goals. Do not market it as a major cycling-performance intervention.

## Tags
stretching, flexibility, ROM

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
