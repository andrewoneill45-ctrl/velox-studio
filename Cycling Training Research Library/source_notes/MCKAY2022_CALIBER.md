# Defining Training and Performance Caliber: A Participant Classification Framework

**Citation:** McKay AKA; Stellingwerff T; Smith ES; et al. (2022). *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*.  
**Type:** Methodological framework  
**Population:** Sport/exercise research participants  
**Directness:** Research interpretation  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1123/ijspp.2021-0451

## What it adds
A six-tier framework distinguishes sedentary through world-class participants, addressing inconsistent use of labels such as “elite”.

## App implication
Store an athlete-calibre tag on every evidence rule so findings from untrained participants are not automatically applied to national/elite riders.

## Tags
evidence, classification, elite

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
