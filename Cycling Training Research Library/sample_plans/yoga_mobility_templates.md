# Yoga and mobility templates

## 8-minute pre-ride mobility
1. ankle rocks ×10/side
2. leg swings ×10 each direction
3. dynamic lunge + reach ×6/side
4. bodyweight squat ×10
5. thoracic rotation ×6/side

## 15-minute post-ride / evening mobility
- hip flexor stretch 2×30–45 s/side
- calf stretch 2×30–45 s/side
- hamstring stretch 2×30–45 s/side
- glute stretch 2×30–45 s/side
- thoracic open-book 8/side
- chest/shoulder opening 60–90 s

## 25-minute easy yoga flow
Child’s pose → cat/cow → low lunge → half split → downward dog → gentle pigeon/figure-four → supine twist → breathing/relaxation.

Keep yoga easy enough that it supports mobility/wellbeing. If it creates significant fatigue or soreness, it should be counted as training stress.
