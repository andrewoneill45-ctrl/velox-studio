# Heart Rate Variability-Guided Training for Enhancing Cardiac-Vagal Modulation, Aerobic Fitness, and Endurance Performance: A Methodological Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis

**Citation:** Manresa-Rocamora A; Sarabia JM; Javaloyes A; et al. (2021). *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** Endurance training studies  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph181910299

## What it adds
HRV-guided training can improve some aerobic/physiological outcomes and may reduce non-response, but performance advantages are modest/inconsistent and protocols differ.

## App implication
Use HRV as one readiness input with individualized baselines and trends. Never cancel or prescribe intensity solely from one morning reading.

## Tags
HRV, readiness, monitoring

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
