# Effectiveness of Recovery Strategies After Training and Competition in Endurance Athletes: An Umbrella Review

**Citation:** Li S; Kempe M; Brink M; et al. (2024). *Sports Medicine - Open*.  
**Type:** Umbrella review  
**Population:** 22 reviews; 63 studies; ~1100 endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-024-00724-6

## What it adds
No recovery modality is consistently superior across outcomes. Some modalities may help particular soreness/perception outcomes, but effects on subsequent performance are inconsistent.

## App implication
Prioritize sleep, nutrition, rest and load management. Treat massage, compression, cold water and similar tools as optional, context-dependent add-ons.

## Tags
recovery, cold-water, compression, massage

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
