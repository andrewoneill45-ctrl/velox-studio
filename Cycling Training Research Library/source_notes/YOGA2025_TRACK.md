# Effect of yoga on selected performance metrics and mental health of track and field athletes: A systematic review

**Citation:** Multiple authors (2025). *Journal of Bodywork and Movement Therapies*.  
**Type:** Systematic review  
**Population:** Track and field athletes  
**Directness:** Athletes but not cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** C  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbmt.2025.08.006

## What it adds
Yoga appears useful for flexibility, balance and some wellbeing/performance measures, but direct cycling-performance evidence remains sparse and study quality is limited.

## App implication
Position yoga as an optional adjunct for mobility, balance, breathing/relaxation and adherence—not a substitute for cycling or strength training.

## Tags
yoga, flexibility, wellbeing

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
