# Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis

**Citation:** Bosquet L; Montpetit J; Arvisais D; Mujika I (2007). *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*.  
**Type:** Meta-analysis  
**Population:** Competitive athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance-heavy mixed sport  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0b013e31806010e0

## What it adds
Classic taper meta-analysis supports approximately two weeks of tapering with a substantial progressive reduction in volume while intensity is maintained.

## App implication
Use as foundational taper logic and cross-check with newer Wang 2023 synthesis.

## Tags
taper, competition

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
