# What is Best Practice for Training Intensity and Duration Distribution in Endurance Athletes?

**Citation:** Seiler S (2010). *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*.  
**Type:** Narrative synthesis  
**Population:** Endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1123/ijspp.5.3.276

## What it adds
A large proportion of successful endurance training is performed at low intensity, with a smaller but important dose of high intensity work.

## App implication
Use low-intensity volume as the foundation and constrain high-intensity frequency to what the athlete can absorb.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
