# Power profiling and the power-duration relationship in cycling: a narrative review

**Citation:** Leo P; Spragg J; Podlogar T; Lawley JS; Mujika I (2022). *European Journal of Applied Physiology*.  
**Type:** Narrative review  
**Population:** Cycling  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-021-04833-y

## What it adds
Structured multi-duration testing and power-duration modelling provide a richer cyclist fingerprint than single-effort metrics; the authors recommend avoiding single-effort prediction trials such as FTP as the sole model.

## App implication
Build a power-duration curve from multiple efforts and recent ride data. Use FTP as a convenient field metric, not the only anchor for zones or progression.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
