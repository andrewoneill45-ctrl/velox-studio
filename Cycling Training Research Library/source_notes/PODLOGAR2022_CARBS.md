# New Horizons in Carbohydrate Research and Application for Endurance Athletes

**Citation:** Podlogar T; Wallis GA (2022). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Review  
**Population:** Endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01757-1

## What it adds
Carbohydrate remains central to prolonged/high-intensity endurance performance. Multiple-transportable carbohydrate strategies enable high exogenous oxidation; intakes above traditional 90 g/h are an emerging advanced option for some athletes/events.

## App implication
Use tiered fuelling: lower amounts for short/easy rides; ~60–90 g/h for prolonged demanding rides; >90 g/h only as an advanced, practiced gut-training strategy rather than a universal target.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
