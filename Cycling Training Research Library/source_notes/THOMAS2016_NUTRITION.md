# Nutrition and Athletic Performance

**Citation:** Thomas DT; Erdman KA; Burke LM (2016). *Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics / ACSM joint position*.  
**Type:** Position statement  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jand.2015.12.006

## What it adds
Energy, carbohydrate, protein, fluid and micronutrient intake should be periodized to support training demands and recovery.

## App implication
Tie fuelling prompts to session duration/intensity and daily training load rather than giving one fixed diet prescription.

## Tags
nutrition, carbohydrate, protein, hydration

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
