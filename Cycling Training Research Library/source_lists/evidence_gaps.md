# Evidence gaps the app should expose honestly

1. **Yoga specifically for cycling performance:** very limited direct evidence.
2. **Exact optimal TID for an individual rider:** no universal polarized/pyramidal winner.
3. **Durability testing standards:** rapidly developing field; protocols are not yet standardized.
4. **Wearable readiness algorithms:** vendor scores are often proprietary and not interchangeable.
5. **Female-athlete phase-based training:** insufficient evidence for deterministic cycle-phase prescriptions.
6. **Bike-fit variables and overuse injury:** direct causal evidence is weaker than common industry claims.
7. **Very high carbohydrate intakes (>90 g/h):** increasingly used/studied, but optimal dose depends on duration, gut training and individual tolerance.
8. **Masters-specific periodization:** principles are clear, but exact age-stratified doses remain individualized.
9. **“Sweet spot” as a unique physiological category:** useful coaching shorthand, not a distinct biological zone.
10. **CTL/TSS optimums:** useful descriptive tools but no scientifically universal target CTL or ramp rate.
