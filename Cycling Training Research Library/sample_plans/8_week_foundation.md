# 8-week foundation block (example 7–10 h/week)

**Pattern:** 2 strength sessions, 1 quality bike session, 1 long ride, remaining riding low intensity. Weeks 4 and 8 reduce volume.

| Week | Key bike | Long ride | Strength | Other riding | Notes |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3×8 min tempo | 2:00 | 2× | 2–3 easy rides | Establish routine |
| 2 | 3×10 min tempo | 2:15 | 2× | easy volume | Add duration |
| 3 | 2×15 min tempo | 2:30 | 2× | easy volume | Progress work duration |
| 4 | 2×10 min tempo | 1:45 | 1–2× lighter | reduced easy volume | Deload |
| 5 | 3×10 min low threshold | 2:30 | 2× | easy volume | Slight specificity |
| 6 | 3×12 min low threshold | 2:45 | 2× | easy volume | Progress |
| 7 | 2×20 min controlled | 3:00 | 2× | easy volume | Longest week |
| 8 | 2×10 min controlled | 2:00 | 1× | reduced | Absorb/retest if needed |
