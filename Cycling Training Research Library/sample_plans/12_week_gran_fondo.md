# 12-week gran fondo / long sportive plan

Priorities: aerobic volume, threshold on climbs, durability, fuelling practice and fatigue resistance.

### Weeks 1–4
Long ride grows 2.5 → 3.5 h; one threshold session; 2× strength; remaining rides easy.

### Weeks 5–8
Long ride 3.5 → 4.5 h; VO2 every 7–10 days; threshold/over-under weekly; 1–2× strength; practise 60–90+ g/h carbohydrate where tolerated.

### Weeks 9–10
Race-specific long rides with climbing/tempo late in the ride; reduce gym to maintenance.

### Week 11
Peak specific ride early in week or prior weekend, then reduce load.

### Week 12
Taper: ~40–60% less volume, keep short intensity touches, preserve normal fuelling and sleep.
