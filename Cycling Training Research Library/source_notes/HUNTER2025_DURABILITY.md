# Durability as an index of endurance exercise performance: Methodological considerations

**Citation:** Hunter B; Maunder E; Jones AM; Gallo G; Muniz-Pumares D (2025). *Experimental Physiology*.  
**Type:** Review  
**Population:** Endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance; highly relevant to cycling  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1113/EP092120

## What it adds
Durability is the resilience of physiological/performance variables to deterioration during or after prolonged exercise. Results are strongly influenced by the fatiguing protocol, nutrition and environment.

## App implication
Track power/HR/decoupling and changes in short-to-medium duration power after accumulated work. Standardize pre-test fuelling and conditions before comparing durability scores.

durability, fatigue-resistance, testing

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
