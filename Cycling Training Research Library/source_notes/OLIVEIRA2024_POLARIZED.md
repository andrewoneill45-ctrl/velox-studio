# Comparison of Polarized Versus Other Types of Endurance Training Intensity Distribution on Athletes’ Endurance Performance: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis

**Citation:** Silva Oliveira P; Boppre G; Fonseca H (2024). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** Endurance athletes; 17 studies, n=437  
**Directness:** Endurance; partially cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02034-z

## What it adds
Polarized training showed a small advantage for VO2peak, particularly in highly trained athletes and interventions under 12 weeks, but was not superior for time trial, time-to-exhaustion, or power/speed at VT2/LT2.

## App implication
Do not hard-code an 80/20 polarized dogma. Offer polarized and pyramidal distributions as evidence-supported templates and select by athlete level, phase, event and response.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
