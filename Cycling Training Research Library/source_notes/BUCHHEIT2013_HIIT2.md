# High-Intensity Interval Training, Solutions to the Programming Puzzle: Part II: Anaerobic Energy, Neuromuscular Load and Practical Applications

**Citation:** Buchheit M; Laursen PB (2013). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Narrative review  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance/applied physiology  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-013-0066-5

## What it adds
Different HIIT formats alter anaerobic contribution, neuromuscular load and recovery cost.

## App implication
Track session purpose and fatigue cost separately. Two sessions with similar TSS can impose very different neuromuscular and metabolic stress.

## Tags
HIIT, anaerobic, neuromuscular

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
