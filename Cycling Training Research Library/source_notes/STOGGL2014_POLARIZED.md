# Polarized training has greater impact on key endurance variables than threshold, high intensity, or high volume training

**Citation:** Stöggl T; Sperlich B (2014). *Frontiers in Physiology*.  
**Type:** Controlled training intervention  
**Population:** Well-trained endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance, mixed modes  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2014.00033

## What it adds
In this intervention, a polarized programme produced strong improvements in several endurance outcomes compared with alternative distributions.

## App implication
Use as supportive—not definitive—evidence for polarized blocks, especially when an athlete is stagnating under threshold-heavy training.

## Tags
polarized, threshold, HIIT

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
