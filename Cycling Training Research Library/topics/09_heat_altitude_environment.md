# Heat, altitude and environment

## Heat
Heat acclimation is evidence-supported. A practical pre-event block can use repeated controlled heat exposures across 7–14 days, often 60–90 min, with progressive safety controls.

App safeguards should account for:
- temperature/humidity;
- hydration access;
- athlete heat history;
- illness/medications;
- excessive HR/RPE drift;
- symptoms of heat illness.

Never prescribe “push through” heat-illness symptoms.

## Altitude
Altitude/hypoxic training can benefit some trained athletes but response is highly individual and implementation is complex. Treat it as an advanced module rather than a core recommendation for recreational riders. Log altitude dose, iron status considerations (clinician-led where relevant), sleep, wellness and performance response.
