# Cardiorespiratory and metabolic consequences of detraining in endurance athletes

**Citation:** Barbieri A; Fuk A; Gallo G; et al. (2024). *Frontiers in Physiology*.  
**Type:** Systematic review  
**Population:** Endurance athletes  
**Directness:** Endurance  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2023.1334766

## What it adds
Training cessation reduces cardiorespiratory and metabolic adaptations; partial training can attenuate losses, while longer cessation causes broader decline.

## App implication
After meaningful time off, automatically reduce prior training targets and rebuild through a re-entry block rather than resuming historical load/FTP workouts unchanged.

## Tags
detraining, return, retraining

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
