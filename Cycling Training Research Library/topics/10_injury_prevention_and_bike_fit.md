# Injury prevention and bike fit

## Core rule
The app should not diagnose injuries or prescribe a bike-fit correction from a generic knee-angle formula.

Systematic-review evidence suggests that many specific fit measures have weak links to overuse injury, whereas symptom history and training-load changes matter.

## Useful app functions
- track pain location, severity and whether it changes pedalling mechanics;
- compare onset with recent volume/intensity/equipment changes;
- temporarily reduce aggravating load;
- flag persistent/recurrent pain for sports physio/medical/bike-fit assessment.

## Red flags
Immediate clinical advice for major trauma, inability to bear weight/use the limb, neurological symptoms, severe swelling/deformity, chest pain, syncope, or other acute concerning symptoms.
