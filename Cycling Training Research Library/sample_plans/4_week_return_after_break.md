# 4-week return after a meaningful break

**Assumption:** previously trained cyclist, medically well, break not caused by unresolved injury/illness. Scale duration to prior history.

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun | Approx focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rest/mobility | 45–60 easy | Strength technique | 60 easy + 4×1 min brisk | Rest | 75–90 easy | 45 easy | Restore frequency |
| 2 | Rest | 60 easy | Strength light/moderate | 3×6 min tempo | Easy/rest | 90–120 easy | 45–60 easy | Add modest load |
| 3 | Rest | 3×8 min tempo/low threshold | Strength moderate | 60–75 easy | Rest | 2 h endurance | 45 recovery | Reintroduce sustained work |
| 4 | Rest | 4×3 min controlled high aerobic | Strength moderate | 60 easy | Rest | 2–2.5 h endurance | 60 easy | First high-aerobic exposure |

Do not use previous FTP blindly. Targets should be RPE/power-response aware until re-tested.
