# Monitoring Athlete Training Loads: Consensus Statement

**Citation:** Bourdon PC; Cardinale M; Murray A; et al. (2017). *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*.  
**Type:** Consensus statement  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Athlete monitoring  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1123/IJSPP.2017-0208

## What it adds
Training-load monitoring should combine internal and external load measures and be interpreted in context; there is no single best metric.

## App implication
Combine power/duration (external load), session RPE/HR (internal load), and subjective state. Avoid automated decisions based on CTL, TSS, HRV or resting HR alone.

## Tags
load, monitoring, RPE, HRV

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
