# A new approach to monitoring exercise training

**Citation:** Foster C; Florhaug JA; Franklin J; et al. (2001). *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*.  
**Type:** Applied monitoring study  
**Population:** Athletes/exercisers  
**Directness:** Training monitoring  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1519/00124278-200102000-00019

## What it adds
Session-RPE provides a simple, low-cost measure of internal training load that can complement objective workload.

## App implication
Collect session RPE after every ride/strength session. It remains valuable even when power, HR and wearable data are available.

## Tags
RPE, load, monitoring

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
