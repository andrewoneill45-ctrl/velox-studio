# Training Session Models in Endurance Sports: A Norwegian Perspective on Best Practice Recommendations

**Citation:** Tønnessen E; Sandbakk Ø; Sandbakk SB; Seiler S; Haugen T (2024). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Pragmatic qualitative best-practice study  
**Population:** 12 world-leading Norwegian endurance coaches across Olympic endurance sports  
**Directness:** Elite endurance; includes road cycling  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02067-4

## What it adds
Successful endurance programmes feature high volumes of low intensity work and controlled, repeatable interval sessions that are often less exhaustive than research intervention protocols.

## App implication
Default hard sessions should usually finish with reserve rather than failure. Protect repeatability and consistency over chasing maximal session scores.

## Tags
best-practice, low-intensity, intervals, elite

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
