# App-ready training principles

1. **Specificity:** training should increasingly resemble the event’s duration, terrain and power demands as competition approaches.
2. **Progressive overload:** add stress only when recent training is being absorbed; do not use a universal 10% rule.
3. **Consistency > hero sessions:** maximize completed quality weeks, not isolated maximal sessions.
4. **Mostly low intensity:** low-intensity volume is the chassis; intensity is targeted.
5. **Limit hard-day density:** most riders need substantial recovery between key interval sessions.
6. **Strength is real training:** count gym work in fatigue/readiness, especially lower-body heavy sessions.
7. **Fuel quality:** under-fuelled quality sessions create noise and can reduce training quality.
8. **Deload responsively:** fixed recovery weeks are a useful template, not a law.
9. **Taper for important events:** cut volume, preserve intensity/frequency.
10. **Update from response:** use completion, RPE, power/HR response, wellbeing and performance trends to modify future load.
11. **Evidence-aware uncertainty:** low-confidence recommendations should be labelled optional/experimental.
12. **Safety overrides performance:** illness, injury and concerning symptoms trump algorithmic progression.
