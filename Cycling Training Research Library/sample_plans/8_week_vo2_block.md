# 8-week VO2-development block

**Not for beginners.** Maintain substantial easy riding; HIIT is the targeted minority of total training.

| Week | Session 1 | Session 2 | Long ride | Strength |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5×3 min | Threshold 3×10 | 2–3 h easy | 1–2× |
| 2 | 6×3 min | Threshold 3×12 | 2.5–3 h easy | 1–2× |
| 3 | 5×4 min | Endurance + 6 sprints | 3 h easy | 1× |
| 4 | 4×3 min | Short threshold 2×10 | 2 h easy | 1× light |
| 5 | 4×5 min | Threshold 3×12 | 3 h easy | 1× |
| 6 | 30/15 microintervals | Threshold 2×20 | 3–3.5 h | 1× |
| 7 | 5×4 min | 30/15 microintervals or event-specific | 3 h easy | 1× |
| 8 | 3×3 min opener | Retest/event | Easy | optional |

If two hard cycling days per week cause declining quality, replace Session 2 with easy endurance.
