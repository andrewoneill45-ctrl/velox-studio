# Readiness adjustment rules — example engine

## Inputs
Use 7–28 day individualized baselines for:
- sleep opportunity and subjective sleep quality;
- fatigue/soreness/motivation (1–5 or 1–7 scales);
- resting HR;
- HRV (if consistently measured);
- prior 3–7 day training load;
- recent key-session completion;
- illness/pain flags.

## Decision example
### Green
No safety flags; 0–1 mildly negative readiness indicators. Proceed as planned.

### Amber
2+ meaningful negative indicators or a clear adverse trend. Keep the session purpose but reduce one stress lever: e.g. -20–30% interval volume, downgrade threshold to endurance, or keep strength load but cut sets.

### Red — performance/fatigue
Multiple severe negative signals plus clear performance deterioration. Replace high intensity with recovery/endurance or rest and reassess.

### Red — medical
Fever, chest pain, syncope, significant breathlessness, acute injury, neurological symptoms, or other concerning illness signs: no training prescription; advise medical assessment.

## Important
Do not derive a green/amber/red state from HRV alone. Subjective state and actual performance response are often highly informative.
