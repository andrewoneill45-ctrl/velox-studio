# The Effects of Menstrual Cycle Phase on Exercise Performance in Eumenorrheic Women: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Citation:** McNulty KL; Elliott-Sale KJ; Dolan E; et al. (2020). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 78 studies; eumenorrheic women  
**Directness:** Female exercise performance; mixed sports  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-020-01319-3

## What it adds
Average performance differences across menstrual-cycle phases were trivial, with large between-study variation and low-quality evidence limiting phase-based prescriptions.

## App implication
Do not auto-periodize training by predicted cycle phase. Allow symptom tracking and individualized adaptation where repeated personal patterns exist.

## Tags
female-athlete, menstrual-cycle, individualization

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
