# Number of Sprint Repetitions in a Sprint Interval Training Session Does Not Moderate Improvements in V̇O₂max With Training: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Citation:** Hutchinson M; Kinghorn DJ; Hall ECR; et al. (2026). *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-analysis  
**Population:** 93 trials across 78 studies; n=1188  
**Directness:** All-out cycling SIT  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.70347

## What it adds
Sprint interval training improved VO2max substantially across studies, but doing more sprint repetitions per session did not explain larger improvements.

## App implication
If prescribing SIT, avoid assuming more repetitions are better. Use the minimum effective, tolerable dose and account for high fatigue and adherence cost.

## Tags
SIT, VO2max, sprint

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
