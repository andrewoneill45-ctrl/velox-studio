# The Influence of Drinking Fluid on Endurance Cycling Performance: A Meta-Analysis

**Citation:** Holland JJ; Skinner TL; Irwin CG; Leveritt MD; Goulet EDB (2017). *Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Meta-analysis  
**Population:** 9 studies; 15 effect estimates; endurance cycling  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-017-0739-6

## What it adds
The effect of drinking during cycling is duration- and intensity-dependent; fluid helped longer moderate-intensity cycling in the included studies but could impose a performance cost in shorter high-intensity trials.

## App implication
Individualize fluid intake by duration, intensity, heat and tolerance rather than mandating maximal replacement. Avoid both excessive dehydration and overdrinking.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
