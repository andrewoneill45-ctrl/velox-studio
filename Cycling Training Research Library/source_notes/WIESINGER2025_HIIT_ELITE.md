# Meta-analyses of the effects of high-intensity interval training in elite athletes—part I: mean effects on various performance measures

**Citation:** Wiesinger HP; Stöggl TL; Haller N; et al. (2025). *Frontiers in Physiology*.  
**Type:** Systematic review + meta-regression  
**Population:** 34 studies; highly trained/elite athletes  
**Directness:** Highly trained and elite endurance  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2024.1486526

## What it adds
HIIT can improve multiple performance measures even in highly trained athletes; more aerobic long-interval formats appear especially useful for VO2max.

## App implication
Scale expected gains by athlete calibre; advanced riders need highly targeted HIIT rather than simply more intensity.

## Tags
HIIT, elite, VO2max

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
