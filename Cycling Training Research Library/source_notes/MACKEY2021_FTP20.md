# What is known about the FTP20 test related to cycling? A scoping review

**Citation:** Mackey J; Horner K (2021). *Journal of Sports Sciences*.  
**Type:** Scoping review  
**Population:** Cycling; 15 studies  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2021.1955515

## What it adds
FTP20 is generally reliable but shows limits of agreement with physiological threshold markers large enough that the measures should not be treated as interchangeable.

## App implication
Label FTP as an estimate/field performance metric. Never claim that 95% of 20-min power precisely equals lactate threshold or maximal metabolic steady state for every rider.

## Tags
FTP, threshold, testing

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
