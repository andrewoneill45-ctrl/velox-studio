# American College of Sports Medicine position stand. Exercise and fluid replacement

**Citation:** Sawka MN; Burke LM; Eichner ER; et al. (2007). *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*.  
**Type:** Position stand  
**Population:** Exercising adults/athletes  
**Directness:** Exercise  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0b013e31802ca597

## What it adds
Hydration strategies should aim to avoid excessive dehydration and overdrinking, with individual sweat rates and environmental demands considered.

## App implication
Estimate sweat rate from pre/post body mass when practical. Never prescribe drinking that causes body-mass gain during exercise; individualize fluid/sodium prompts by conditions and tolerance.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
