# Strength training improves performance and pedaling characteristics in elite cyclists

**Citation:** Rønnestad BR; Hansen J; Hollan I; Ellefsen S (2015). *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*.  
**Type:** 25-week controlled training intervention  
**Population:** 16 young elite cyclists  
**Directness:** Cycling-specific  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** No / check publisher access  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.12257

## What it adds
Long-term heavy strength training improved several cycling performance determinants and performance outcomes in well-trained cyclists.

## App implication
Strength gains need months, not a token 2–3 week block. Make strength a year-round component with development and maintenance phases.

## Tags
strength, cycling, long-term

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
