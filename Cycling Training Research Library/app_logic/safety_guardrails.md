# Safety guardrails

The app should not:
- diagnose injury, RED-S, overtraining syndrome, eating disorders or cardiac disease;
- encourage exercise through fever/systemic illness;
- encourage deliberate dehydration;
- prescribe extreme caloric restriction;
- prescribe unregulated drugs/hormones;
- guarantee that a bike-fit angle will cure pain;
- use wearables as medical devices unless validated/approved for that purpose.

The app should escalate to professional care for:
- chest pain, fainting/syncope, severe or unexpected breathlessness;
- signs of heat illness;
- major trauma or neurological symptoms;
- persistent/recurrent pain altering movement;
- prolonged unexplained performance decline/fatigue;
- concerning RED-S/eating-disorder features;
- return from significant illness/injury where clinical clearance is relevant.
