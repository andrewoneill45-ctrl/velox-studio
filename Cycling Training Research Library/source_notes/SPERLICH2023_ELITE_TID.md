# The proportional distribution of training by elite endurance athletes at different intensities during different phases of the season

**Citation:** Sperlich B; Matzka M; Holmberg HC (2023). *Frontiers in Sports and Active Living*.  
**Type:** Review of elite-athlete training distributions  
**Population:** 437 elite athletes; 175 reported TIDs  
**Directness:** Elite endurance; includes cycling  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2023.1258585

## What it adds
Across reported elite-athlete training distributions, pyramidal and polarized patterns were both common and varied by sport and phase of season.

## App implication
Allow intensity distribution to evolve across a season: pyramidal base/build is common; more polarized patterns may emerge in some phases.

## Tags
elite, TID, season, periodization

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
