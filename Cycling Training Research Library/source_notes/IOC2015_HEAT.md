# Consensus recommendations on training and competing in the heat

**Citation:** Racinais S; Alonso JM; Coutts AJ; et al. (2015). *British Journal of Sports Medicine*.  
**Type:** Consensus statement  
**Population:** Athletes  
**Directness:** Athletes/endurance  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2015-094915

## What it adds
Heat acclimation is an effective preparation strategy; repeated heat exposures over roughly 1–2 weeks produce substantial cardiovascular and thermoregulatory adaptation.

## App implication
For hot-event preparation, offer a heat-acclimation module using progressive, controlled exposure, hydration monitoring and conservative safety limits.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
