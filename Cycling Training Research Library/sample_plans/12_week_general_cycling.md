# 12-week general cycling performance plan (example 8–12 h/week)

Designed for a trained rider seeking broad improvement rather than a single race specialty.

## Weeks 1–4 — foundation
- Tue: sustained tempo/threshold progression
- Thu: endurance + short sprints every second week
- Sat: long endurance 2.5–3.5 h
- Sun: easy endurance
- Strength: 2×/week
- Week 4: reduce cycling volume ~25–35%

## Weeks 5–8 — build
- Tue: VO2 session (e.g. 5×4 → 4×5)
- Thu: threshold (3×10 → 3×15)
- Sat: long endurance; every second week add 20–30 min late tempo
- Sun: easy/recovery
- Strength: 1–2×/week
- Week 8: reduced load

## Weeks 9–11 — specific performance
- Tue: VO2 or microintervals
- Thu: event-specific threshold/over-under work
- Sat: 3–4 h endurance with selective late work
- Sun: easy
- Strength: ~1× maintenance

## Week 12 — test/taper
Reduce total volume ~40–50%, keep two short intensity touches, then complete the chosen field test/event.
