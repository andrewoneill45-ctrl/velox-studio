# International society of sports nutrition position stand: caffeine and exercise performance

**Citation:** Guest NS; VanDusseldorp TA; Nelson MT; et al. (2021). *Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition*.  
**Type:** Position stand  
**Population:** Exercise/athletic performance  
**Directness:** Athletes  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-020-00383-4

## What it adds
Caffeine can improve endurance and other performance outcomes for many athletes; common effective doses are around 3–6 mg/kg, with some benefit at lower doses and greater side-effect risk at high doses.

## App implication
If included, make caffeine optional, individualized and bounded by sleep, tolerance, medical considerations and competition rules. Do not normalize high dosing.

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
