# Consensus Statements—Optimizing Performance of the Elite Athlete (female-athlete section)

**Citation:** International expert consensus group (2025). *Consensus publication*.  
**Type:** Consensus  
**Population:** Elite athletes  
**Directness:** Elite athlete  
**Evidence grade:** A  
**Open access:** Yes  
**DOI / source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12334928/

## What it adds
Current evidence is insufficient to prescribe training, nutrition or injury-prevention changes by menstrual phase; athlete-specific symptoms and clinically valid information should guide adjustments.

## App implication
Treat cycle tracking as context, not a deterministic training engine.

## Tags
female-athlete, consensus, menstrual-cycle

## Interpretation caution
This is a concise evidence note, not a reproduction of the paper. Apply findings according to athlete calibre, intervention duration, sex representation, event demands and study limitations. Where the source is not cycling-specific, directness is explicitly downgraded.
