# Strength templates

## Phase 0 — technique / adaptation (2–4 weeks)
2×/week. 2–3 sets of 6–10 reps at easy/moderate loads. Learn movement, keep 3–4 reps in reserve.

## Phase 1 — heavy development (8–16 weeks)
2×/week.

### Session A
- squat or leg press: 3×4–6
- Romanian deadlift: 3×4–6
- split squat: 2–3×5–6/side
- calf raise: 3×6–10
- Pallof press or dead bug: 2×8–12

### Session B
- trap-bar deadlift or alternative hinge: 3×3–5
- step-up: 3×5–6/side
- leg press/squat variation: 2–3×4–6
- hamstring curl: 2×6–10
- side plank: 2 sets

Use long rests (2–4 min on heavy compounds), crisp technique, and usually stop with 1–3 reps in reserve.

## Phase 2 — cycling build maintenance
1×/week. Keep intensity fairly heavy, cut exercise/set volume ~30–50%.

## Race week
Either omit heavy strength or place a very low-volume maintenance exposure early enough that soreness does not affect the event.
